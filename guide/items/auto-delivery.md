# Auto-delivery of items to the shop

## What is this?

On a schedule, the bot increases or sets the amount of an item in the shop's stock.

## Discord

In the item's **Shop** section, choose daily, weekly, or monthly auto-delivery.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (7).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

### Auto-delivery type

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (8).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

| Type | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Increases the amount | Adds N to the current stock |
| Sets the amount | Sets the stock to exactly N |

{% hint style="info" %}
Moscow time (MSK):

- daily — 03:25
- weekly — 03:30
- monthly — 03:35
{% endhint %}
