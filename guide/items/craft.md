# Crafting an item

Up to 10 recipes per item and up to 10 ingredients per recipe. Players craft with `/craft`.

To create a recipe in Discord, go to the "Creation" section and click ![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (10).png>)

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (9).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

The bot will offer to add currency or an item to the recipe

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (11).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

Add the required items to the recipe and click the "Next" button

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (14).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

## Recipe settings

Recipe known - if disabled, the item cannot be crafted with this recipe. For the recipe to be known, you need to create an item that teaches the recipe when used.

Permission - a permission preset

Cooldown - how long until this recipe can be used again to craft the item

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (15).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

***

After that, the recipe will be created and the item can be crafted with the command [/craft <item name>](../../commands/inventory.md)

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (16).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (17).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

An unknown recipe is learned through an item with the "Learn recipe" action in [Using the item](use.md).
