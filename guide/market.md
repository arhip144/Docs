# Market

## What is this?

The market is trading between players (items and roles for currency/other resources). It differs from the server shop `/shop`.

{% hint style="warning" %}
The market is available with [premium](../premium.md). The item must have the "Sellable on the market" property.
{% endhint %}

## Discord

Command [/market](../commands/general.md) (premium).

The channel and listing storage duration settings are in [/manager-settings](settings.md) → Market.
